# 3D Solar System Animation

<a href="https://claude.ai/public/artifacts/4d1846f5-724d-4936-ba8e-fa4f8970fe17" target="_blank">Visit here...</a>

<h2>Features</h2>
<h2>Visual Elements:</h2>
<ul>
  <li>☀️ The Sun at the center with a glowing effect</li>
  <li>🪐 All 8 planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune)</li>
  <li>💫 Saturn's iconic rings</li>
  <li>⭐ 5,500+ stars in the background with varying sizes and colors</li>
  <li>🌌 Realistic 3D lighting and shadows</li>
</ul>


<h2>Interactive Controls:</h2>
<ul>
  <li>🖱️ Drag to rotate your viewpoint</li>
  <li>🖱️ Scroll to zoom in/out</li>
  <li>🖱️ Hover over planets to see their names</li>
  <li>🖱️ Click any planet or the Sun to see detailed information</li>
  <li>⏸️ Pause/Resume button</li>
  <li>🎚️ Orbit Speed slider (0.1x to 3x)</li>
  <LI>🎚️ Planet Rotation slider (0x to 5x)</LI>
</ul>

<h2>Educational Information</h2>
<p>Each planet displays:</p>
<ul>
  Diameter
Distance from the Sun
Day length (rotation period)
Year length (orbital period)
Number of moons
Interesting description
</ul>
